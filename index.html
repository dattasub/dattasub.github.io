About

I'm a senior IT QA engineer and software engineer with more than 20 years in IT. At Cotality (formerly CoreLogic) I'm one of two QA engineers working with 12 hazard scientists to validate catastrophe risk models for earthquake, flood and storm surge, hurricane, severe convective storm, winter storm, wind and wildfire. I test against hundreds of millions of exposure records, and I've helped build an in-house suite of more than 3,500 automated test cases.

Before moving into QA, I spent over ten years as a software engineer and SQL Server developer. I built SSIS pipelines that loaded 700 to 900 GB a day into a 7 TB data warehouse, directed a 10-person offshore team, and tuned slow production code, in one case cutting a stored procedure from 3 minutes to 3 seconds. Earlier, I led a 16-person development team for clients in the US and India.

People bring me the hard data and database problems, the ones that need someone to dig past the surface and find the root cause. I use AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex, GitHub Copilot and Gemini to automate data work, extend test coverage and track down bugs in T-SQL and Python. I've also mentored and onboarded five colleagues.

I'm looking for QA and software engineering roles, including SQL Server and data work. I'm open to remote work and to relocating.
